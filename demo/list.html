List grid demo

New in v14: the mode option ('top' | 'float' | 'list' | 'compact') which replaces the old float boolean.

In mode: 'list' items are continuously re-flowed in sequential (row-major) order, like a re-orderable list: dropping an item on another one takes its place and everything else shifts over, instead of being pushed down. Use the dropdown to compare all 4 modes, and see list advanced demo for items of different sizes.
